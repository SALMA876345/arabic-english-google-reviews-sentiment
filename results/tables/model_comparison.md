# Model Comparison (Phase 3)

**Note on `is_binary_or_partial_label_task` rows (currently: Jordan auxiliary):** this source has zero Neutral examples (confirmed, see known_limitations.md #12). The `macro_f1`/`precision_macro`/`recall_macro` columns for these rows are the observed-classes-only (Positive/Negative) values, the task-appropriate numbers, NOT the standard 3-class metrics forcing a phantom zero-support Neutral class -- those misleading 3-class numbers are preserved separately in the `*_CAUTION_forces_3class_incl_absent_class` columns for transparency, but must never be quoted as THE Jordan result.

**Note on `LoRA_adapted_NOT_full_finetune`:** per docs/decisions.md D14, these rows (E1_xlmr, E1_marbert, E2_xlmr, E5_*, E4_*) are LoRA-adapted (only ~0.3% of parameters trainable), not standard fully fine-tuned transformers, due to a confirmed CPU/RAM constraint. They are NOT directly equivalent to a fully fine-tuned model and may underperform one -- do not cite these as representative transformer performance without this caveat.

**Note on `test_label_provenance`:** `rating_derived_NOT_human_verified` means the test labels were converted from star ratings, not assigned by a human reader of the text -- per docs/label_provenance_policy.md, these are not acceptable as final evaluation ground truth without the pending manual-validation slice (docs/annotation_guidelines.md).
